The grid targets WCAG 2.2 AA. It always builds a valid ARIA tree with accessible names. With `enableKeyboardNavigation` it becomes a `grid` with arrow-key navigation; without it, it is a `table` and every control is reachable with Tab. Everything you can do by dragging, you can also do from a menu.
